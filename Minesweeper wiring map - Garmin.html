Minesweeper wiring map: Garmin heart-rate version

Arduino Uno · ILI9341 screen through a 74VHC86 · DFPlayer Mini · joystick · flag button · Garmin Forerunner 265S → ESP32 bridge. Starts from the current breadboard (74VHC86 screen shifting + analog pulse sensor) and marks every change. Use with HR_SOURCE_BLE 1 in config.h. Tick each wire as you place it; ticks stay in your browser.

What changes from the current breadboard (NEW add, REMOVE take out, everything else stays):
  1. NEWESP32 DevKit V1, powered from its own USB cable.
  2. NEWESP32 GND → Uno GND rail. Required: the serial signal needs a shared ground.
  3. NEWESP32 GPIO17 (TX2) → Uno D0 (RX). One wire, no resistor or shifter. Unplug it from D0 every time you upload to the Uno.
  4. REMOVEPulse sensor (+, −, S → A2). Optional: in Garmin mode it is ignored, so you can leave it in to switch back with HR_SOURCE_BLE 0.
  5. No change to the screen, 74VHC86, RESET divider, DFPlayer, joystick or flag button.
Before you start: unplug the power bank and USB while wiring. Every part shares one ground, including the ESP32. Never connect the ESP32's 3.3V or 5V/VIN pin to the Uno's rails while both boards are on USB.

6. Heart rate: Garmin + ESP32 bridge CHANGED

ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin DOIT board): GPIO17 is the pin printed TX2 (sometimes D17). There are GND pins on both sides; any of them works.

8. Check before powering on

  • Nothing from the 5V rail touches the 3.3V rail, and neither touches GND.
  • 74VHC86 notch faces the right way, pin 14 on 3.3 V, pin 7 and all four B inputs on GND.
  • RESET goes through the divider, not straight from D8.
  • ESP32 GND is on the GND rail; its 3.3V and VIN pins are not connected to anything.
  • GPIO17 → D0 wire is out while uploading to the Uno, and back in afterwards.
  • HR_SOURCE_BLE in include/config.h is 1, and the ESP32 has the bridge (pio run -e esp32_hr -t upload).
