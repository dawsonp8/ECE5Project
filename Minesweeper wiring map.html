Minesweeper wiring map

Arduino Uno · ILI9341 screen · DFPlayer Mini · joystick · flag button · pulse sensor. Matches the pin map in the build spec. Tick each wire as you place it; ticks stay in your browser.

Before you start: unplug the power bank and USB while wiring. Every part shares one ground. The screen's control pins must go through the 74HC4050, never straight from the Uno (5 V signals can damage a 3.3 V screen).

7. Check before powering on

  • Nothing from the 5V rail touches the 3.3V rail. Check with a multimeter (continuity mode) between the two rails: it must not beep.
  • No continuity between 5V and GND, or 3.3V and GND.
  • 74HC4050 notch faces the right way and pin 1 is on 3.3 V.
  • 470 µF cap stripe goes to GND.
  • First power-up: USB from the laptop, not the power bank, so you can watch Serial Monitor. Wire and test one part at a time in the same order as the build spec (screen, controls, audio, pulse).
